      How the match works

      1. 1

        Read your CV

        Skills, languages, tools and years of experience are detected on your device. You can fix them.

      2. 2

        Collect offers

        Live offers from several job boards, cleaned, de-duplicated and located on a map.

      3. 3

        Score each offer

        Skills overlap (45%), job title vs target role (25%), text similarity (18%), experience fit (12%). Offers outside your field sink or are hidden.

      4. 4

        Best on top

        Filter by contract and distance. The highest match is always first.
